# PJ3 – Arris Patch

DE: Kantenflicken · JP: kado-umeki

An L-section strip taken off one arris over a length and replaced. The arris
is where a post weathers first: it catches rain, dries slowest and loses
material long before the core is touched.

`kind: "patch"` · restores **surface** · does not carry along the member

## Geometry

Four half-space cuts, one intersect group: inboard faces at 22% from each of
two adjacent sides, bounded in y, **open at two faces**.

| | canonical | of section |
|---|---|---|
| inset from each face | 0.22 | 22% |
| length | 1.50 | 1.5 × |
| corner removed | 0.28 × 0.28 | |

Kept fraction **0.962**.

## When to reach for it

Weathered or rotted arrises on exposed timber — the commonest damage on a
Fachwerk post and the one a full splice answers most wastefully. Long and
shallow rather than short and deep, so it follows damage that runs with the
member instead of across it.

Being open on **two** faces it is the easiest patch in the set to fit: the
block drops in diagonally and needs no clearance along the beam.

## Fabrication

Two long rip cuts along the grain and two cross-grain shoulders. The rips can
be run with a circular saw against a fence; the shoulders are the fussy part.
Fit the strip from the corner, glue and cramp inward from both faces.

Watch the grain direction of the replacement strip — an arris patch is highly
visible and a mismatch reads immediately.
