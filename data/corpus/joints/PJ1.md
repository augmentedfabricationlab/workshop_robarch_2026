# PJ1 – Surface Patch

DE: Flicken · JP: umeki (埋木)

The smallest repair in the catalogue. A shallow rectangular pocket in one
face, filled with a block of matching timber. Removes a local soft spot and
nothing else.

`kind: "patch"` · restores **surface** · does not carry along the member

## Geometry

Five half-space cuts, one intersect group: bounded in x, bounded in y, floor
at 22% depth, open at the +z face.

| | canonical | of section |
|---|---|---|
| width | ±0.28 | 56% |
| length | 0.70 | 70% |
| depth | 0.22 | 22% |

Kept fraction **0.972** — it takes almost nothing.

## When to reach for it

Surface decay, a knot that has fallen out, insect flight holes, a redundant
bolt hole. Anywhere the defect is shallower than a quarter of the section and
the wood around it is sound.

Not for anything that has lost capacity: PJ1 restores appearance and sheds
water, and that is all.

## Fabrication

Two cross-grain shoulders sawn to depth, the floor wasted out with the grain
and pared flat, the block fitted from the face. In situ with hand tools, no
end-grain work. Cut the block fat and pare to the pocket.
