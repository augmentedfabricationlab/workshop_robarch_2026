Three days, structured as one loop repeated at increasing resolution: **read the damage → propose a repair → execute it → measure what actually happened**.

<span class="tbd">draft — times and day boundaries to be confirmed</span>

## Day 1 — Reading the structure

| Time | Phase |
|------|-------|
| 09:30–11:00 | Introduction: motivation, "damage as prompt", workflow |
| 11:00–12:00 | Tour of the wall and mobile robot |
| 12:00–13:00 | LUNCH BREAK
| 13:00–14:00 | Capture: using the web-based app, producting photographs, notes, measurements, scans |
| 14:00–17:00 | Generate and compare at least three alternative repair strategies, one of them robotic |

**Deliverable.** A verified Assembly Model and multiple Action Models, representing alternative repair strategies.

## Day 2 — From strategy to toolpath

| Time | Phase |
|------|-------|
| 09:00–11:00 | Comparing the repair strategies, discussion and further development |
| 11:00–12:00 | Joint selection and parametrisation; derive the cut geometry |
| 12:00–13:00 | LUNCH BREAK
| 13:00–15:00 | Robotic execution: localization, marking - Manual execution: material removal |
| 15:00–17:00 | Scan-after-cut: measure the result against the model, and reconstructing part |

**Deliverable.** An explicit, ordered repair sequence with each step allocated to human or robot and detailed repair geometry.

## Day 3 — Execution and evaluation

| Time | Phase |
|------|-------|
| 09:00–11:00 | Robotic execution: milling and fitting|
| 11:00–12:00 | Comparative presentation and discussion |
| 12:00–13:00 | LUNCH BREAK


**Deliverable.** A repaired wall, and the digital record of how the decision was reached and fabricated.
